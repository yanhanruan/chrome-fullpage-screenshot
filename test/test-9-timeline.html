📅 Company Development Timeline

The complete journey from founding to now

2010

Company Founded

Three founders started their entrepreneurial journey in a garage, with an initial team of only 5 people, focusing on mobile app development.

2011

First Product Released

After a year of hard work, our first product was officially launched, gaining support from 10,000 early users.

2012

Angel Round Funding

Successfully secured $5 million in Angel round funding, the team expanded to 20 people, and began professional operations.

2013

Users Surpass 1 Million

The number of users broke the 1 million mark, daily active users reached 300,000, becoming a new star in the industry.

2014

Series A Funding

Completed $20 million Series A funding, began laying out multiple product lines, and the team increased to 80 people.

2015

International Expansion

Started entering international markets, setting up offices in the US and Europe, and the product supports 15 languages.

2016

Series B Funding

Obtained $80 million Series B funding, valuation exceeded $500 million, officially becoming a unicorn candidate.

2017

Product Matrix Improved

Launched 5 new products, forming a complete product matrix, covering mobile, web, and desktop ends.

2018

Technical Innovation

Established an AI lab to begin deep exploration in the fields of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

2019

Users Surpass 10 Million

Global users broke the 10 million mark, becoming one of the platforms with the largest user scale in the industry.

2020

Series C Funding

Completed $250 million Series C funding, valuation reached $2 billion, officially joining the unicorn ranks.

Strategic Acquisition

Acquired two startups, integrated high-quality resources, and further consolidated market position.
